About Golden Ember

Where fire, flavour and hospitality meet

Our Story

Created for unforgettable dining

Golden Ember began with a simple idea: serve refined food without losing warmth and personality. Every menu, table and detail is designed to make guests feel welcome.

Our kitchen combines classic techniques with contemporary presentation, while our service team focuses on comfort, timing and genuine hospitality.
